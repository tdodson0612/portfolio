Questions

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You answer a few questions by email, I build the site, and you review it before it goes live. Changes are as simple as sending me an email.

Who owns my website?

Your business name, logo, photos and text always belong to you. On a monthly plan, the website files are yours once all 12 payments are made. On a one-time project, they're yours once it's paid off.

What happens after 12 months on a monthly plan?

Plans priced above $39 drop to $39 a month to keep your site hosted, with the same monthly update time. Or you can take your files and move the site anywhere you like.

When are payments due?

On monthly plans, the first payment is due when your site goes live, and after that on the 1st of each month. I take Venmo, Cash App, Facebook Pay or a check.

What if I need to pause or stop?

You can pause for up to 2 months a year with no fees, as long as you let me know before the due date. To end a plan early, give 30 days' notice by email; the site comes down, and you can buy the files and domain for the remaining balance.

What if I miss a payment?

There's a 60-day grace period. After that, a $10 late fee applies for each missed month (never more than $50 per bill) and updates pause; after 90 days the site can go offline until you're caught up. On the $5 plan, the page goes offline until the account is current.

Do you only work in Oregon?

I'm based in North Bend, Oregon, but everything happens by email, so I work with clients anywhere in the U.S.
